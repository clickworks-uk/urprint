Cookie policy

What this site stores on your device, why it is there, and how to change it.

Not yet legal advice. This page was drafted from how the website actually behaves in code. Items marked [UR Print to confirm] are facts only UR Print holds. Have a solicitor or your insurer's legal helpline review it before you rely on it.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. The short version

This website does not currently set any cookies. It loads no analytics, no advertising tags and no third-party scripts of any kind. The only thing it stores is a small amount of data in your browser's local storage so that your basket works.

If we later add analytics or marketing, nothing in those categories will run until you have opted in, and you will see a consent banner before anything is set.

2. Cookies and local storage

A cookie is a small file a site stores on your device and sends back to the server with each request. Local storage is similar but stays on your device and is never sent automatically. UK law (PECR) treats both the same way: anything that is not strictly necessary needs your consent first.

4. Third-party content

The site embeds nothing from other domains — no maps, no video players, no social widgets, no hosted fonts. The map in the footer is a picture we drew, and the link beside it only contacts Google if you click it. Our social icons are plain links.

5. Changing your mind

Use the link, which is in the footer of every page. Your choice is stored for 12 months, after which we will ask again.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat that as a refusal of all optional categories and the preferences dialog will tell you so.

You can also clear everything from your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Clearing site data will empty your basket, because that is where it is kept.
