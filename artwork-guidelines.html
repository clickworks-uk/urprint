Artwork guidelines

Everything our studio needs to print your job right first time. Supply a file that follows these notes and we will have it on press the same day.

Every file is checked by a person, not a robot. If your artwork is missing bleed, is low resolution or uses the wrong colour mode, we will call you before we print — never after.

The five essentials

ItemWhat we need
Bleed3mm on every edge (5mm on booklets and boxes)
Safe areaKeep text and logos at least 4mm inside the trim line
Resolution300dpi at final size — 150dpi is fine for banners and boards
ColourCMYK, not RGB. Rich black = C40 M30 Y30 K100
FontsEmbedded in the PDF, or converted to outlines

File formats we accept

  • PDF — press-ready PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 is ideal, with crop marks and bleed
  • AI / EPS — Illustrator files with fonts outlined and images embedded
  • PNG / JPG / TIFF — flattened at 300dpi, with bleed already included
  • Canva — export as "PDF Print" with crop marks and bleed switched on

Files up to 100MB upload directly on the product page. Anything larger, email a WeTransfer link to info@urprint.co.uk with your order number.

Product-specific notes

Business cards & spot UV

Supply the spot UV layer as a separate page or layer filled 100% K, named "SpotUV". Keep UV elements at least 1mm away from the trim. Soft-touch and velvet laminates slightly darken solid colours — allow for it, or ask for a wet proof.

Folded leaflets & booklets

Send flat artwork with fold positions marked on a separate layer. Booklet page counts must be a multiple of four for saddle stitching. Allow extra margin on the inside of a roll fold — the inner panel is trimmed narrower.

Banners, boards and signage

Build large-format artwork at 25% or 50% scale at 300dpi, or full size at 100–150dpi. Keep critical content 100mm away from eyelet positions on banners, and clear of the fixing points on rigid boards.

Stickers & labels

Supply a cutter guide as a vector path on its own layer named "CutContour", set to a 100% magenta spot colour. Leave 2mm of bleed outside the cut path.

Garments

Vector artwork is best for embroidery — we will digitise it free on orders of 10 or more. For DTF printing, supply a transparent PNG at 300dpi sized to the finished print area.
