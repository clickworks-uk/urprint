2. What this website collects

We have deliberately kept this website light. As at the date above it loads no third-party scripts, sets no cookies and sends no data to any analytics provider. Fonts are served from our own domain rather than Google Fonts, so visiting the site does not disclose your IP address to a font provider.

2.1 Information stored on your own device

The site keeps a small amount of data in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and we cannot read it remotely.

2.2 Information you send us

Where the site offers a quote request, an order or an artwork upload, the information you type is used only to respond to that request. That will typically be your name, business name, email address, telephone number, delivery address and anything you tell us about the job.

Current status: the forms on this site are not yet connected to a mail or order system, so nothing you type is transmitted anywhere. When that changes, this section and section 4 must be updated to name the providers involved.

2.3 Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard server logs when a page is requested: IP address, the page requested, the time, the referring page and your browser's user-agent string. These are used to deliver the site securely, diagnose faults and detect abuse.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase it, where we have no continuing reason to keep it.
  • Restrict how we use it while a concern is resolved.
  • Port it to another provider in a machine-readable format.
  • Object to processing we carry out under legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis we relied on.

Email info@urprint.co.uk and we will respond within one month. There is no charge for a reasonable request.

8. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with HSTS enabled, and sends the X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options and Referrer-Policy headers. Artwork and order handling will need their own controls once connected; see section 2.2.

9. Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes to how we use your data will be brought to your attention on the site.
